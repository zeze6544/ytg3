NARCØDEX privacy

Browsing the public archive does not require an account. NARCØDEX does not run advertising and does not sell personal information.

If you create an account, the app stores your email address, a user id, the files you save, and a private list of files you open while signed in. That data is used only to show you those lists. It is stored with our database provider, Supabase.

You can sign out inside the app. To delete an account, open a request at github.com/zeze6544/ytg3/issues.

The iOS app does not request location, contacts, photos, camera, or tracking permission. Reference links open in the system browser.

This archive is not medical advice.
